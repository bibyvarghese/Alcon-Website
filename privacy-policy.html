Privacy / Alcon

01 / Collection

Information we receive

When you contact Alcon by email, telephone, or another enquiry channel, we receive the information you choose to provide. This may include your name, company, role, contact details, project requirements, and files or information included in your message.

02 / Use

How we use information

We use enquiry information to respond to questions, prepare project information, provide quotations, coordinate meetings, support customers, and communicate about services you have requested. We may also keep business correspondence so that we can maintain accurate project records.

03 / Sharing

When information is shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share relevant information with trusted service providers, professional advisers, project partners, or authorities where necessary to respond to your request, operate the business, protect rights, or comply with the law.

04 / Services

Cookies and third parties

This static website does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. Pages may load Google Fonts, the Tailwind CDN, Google Maps, and externally hosted images. Those providers may receive standard technical request data under their own privacy policies.

05 / Retention

Keeping information

We retain enquiry and business correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, to manage active or potential projects, meet business obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain appropriate records.

06 / Your choices

Access and correction

You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask us to delete information where applicable. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

07 / Security

Protecting information

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information in our care. No internet transmission or email service can be guaranteed completely secure, so please avoid sending highly sensitive information through ordinary email.

08 / Updates

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services, or legal obligations change. The current version will remain available on this page with the latest revision date.
